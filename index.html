Topic 1 • 數據異質性 / Data Heterogeneity

🔍 假設第 3 組與第 7 組的折線走向完全相反，你會如何驗證？

If Group 3 and Group 7 have completely opposite line trends, how would you verify it?

如果出現兩組數據走勢矛盾，我們應該直接刪除異常組別，還是重新檢查操作細節？
If two groups show contradictory trends, should we discard the outlier immediately, or re-examine experimental details?

💡 核心：激發學生思考重複驗證 (Replication) 與異常值處理。
Core: Inspire scientific thinking on Replication & Outlier handling.

🪜 鷹架引導步驟 / Scaffolding Steps：

1. 基礎觀察 (Basic Observation)：比較兩組在 150 cm³ 的相差秒數，能否一眼看出離群走勢？
Compare the time difference at 150 cm³ between both groups. Can you spot the outlier trend?

2. 變因排查 (Variable Check)：兩組的水溫、方糖質量是否確實相同？有沒有同學把方糖戳碎了？
Were water temperature and sugar mass truly identical? Did anyone crush the sugar cube?

3. 科學決策 (Scientific Decision)：如果無法判定誰出錯，科學家會：A. 刪除異常值 B. 算平均 C. 重複實驗？為什麼？
If unsure which is wrong, would scientists: A. Discard outlier, B. Average them, or C. Replicate test? Why?

Topic 2 • 實驗工程設計 / Experimental Engineering

🛠️ 如果要設計一個完全「零人為誤差」的溶解實驗，你會如何改進裝置？

To design a "Zero Human Error" dissolving experiment, how would you improve the apparatus?

想像你可以使用任何 STEM 設備（例如磁力攪拌器、光學傳感器），你會如何改進這套實驗？
Imagine using any STEM apparatus (e.g. magnetic stirrers, optical sensors), how would you optimize the setup?

💡 核心：引導 STEM 工程思維與自動化檢測概念。
Core: Guide STEM engineering design & automated detection concepts.

🪜 鷹架引導步驟 / Scaffolding Steps：

1. 鎖定人為誤差 (Identify Human Error)：實驗中，最難用肉眼和雙手精確控制的步驟是什麼？（攪拌均勻度與終點判斷）
Which steps are hardest to control manually? (Stirring consistency & visual endpoint judgment)

2. 工具替代 (Tool Replacement)：若引入實驗室「磁力攪拌器」，如何精確固定每分鐘轉速（RPM）？
If using a magnetic stirrer, how would it fix the constant RPM?

3. 自動檢測 (Automated Detection)：怎樣利用光學濁度傳感器（Light Sensor），讓儀器自動判定方糖已 100% 溶解？
How could a light/turbidity sensor automatically detect when sugar is 100% dissolved?

Topic 3 • 極限觀察 / Limit Thinking

🌊 若將水量無限增加至 5000 cm³，溶解時間會無限制地變成 0 秒嗎？

If water volume increases infinitely to 5000 cm³, will dissolving time drop to 0 seconds?

從數據曲線的趨勢來看，水量加速溶解效果是否存在物理極限？
Looking at the curve trend, does the dissolving acceleration effect have a physical limit?

💡 核心：培養極限思維 (Limit Thinking) 與外推法批判。
Core: Cultivate Limit Thinking & extrapolation critique.

🪜 鷹架引導步驟 / Scaffolding Steps：

1. 觀察斜率 (Observe Gradient)：水量由 50 至 100 cm³ 減少的時間，比 150 至 200 cm³ 減少的時間是多還是少？
Is the time saved from 50 to 100 cm³ larger or smaller than that from 150 to 200 cm³?

2. 物理常識 (Physical Reality)：即使扔進太平洋（極大水量），水分子拆解方糖晶格需要時間嗎？能否為 0 秒？
Even in an ocean, does water take time to break sugar crystal bonds? Can it ever be 0s?

3. 外推批判 (Extrapolation Critique)：用有限的數據曲線去預測無窮大的極端情況，需要注意什麼限制？
What are the risks of using limited data points to predict extreme conditions?
